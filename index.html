01 The Conference Fifth Edition

Five editions in, TGSMUN has grown into Hyderabad's most spirited stage for young diplomacy.

Our delegates don't just debate — they research, caucus, draft and build consensus under pressure, developing the confidence and empathy that real-world diplomacy demands.

Whether it's your first placard or your fifteenth gavel, there is a seat across our senior and junior committees with your name on it.
