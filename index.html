01 The Conference Est. 2019

Six sessions in, TGSMUN has grown from a single hall into the region's most spirited stage for young diplomacy.

Our delegates don't just debate — they research, caucus, draft and build consensus under pressure, developing the confidence and empathy that real-world diplomacy demands.

Whether it's your first placard or your fifteenth gavel, there is a seat in one of our seven committees with your name on it.
