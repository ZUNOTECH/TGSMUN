About the Conference

A tradition of diplomacy since 2019

TGSMUN is the flagship Model United Nations conference of TGS, bringing together the brightest young minds from schools across the region. Over six editions, it has grown from a single-hall simulation into a two-day conference spanning seven committees.

Our delegates don't just debate — they research, negotiate, draft and build consensus, developing the confidence and empathy that real-world diplomacy demands. Whether you're a first-time delegate or a seasoned award winner, TGSMUN has a committee for you.

Join the Sixth Edition

Dear Delegates, it is my honour to welcome you to the sixth edition of TGSMUN. This year, our theme — “Dialogue over Division” — asks each of us to listen before we speak, and to seek common ground before claiming victory. I look forward to watching you rise to that challenge.
