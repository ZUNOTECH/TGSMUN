01 Letter from the Secretary-General Fifth Edition

Five editions in, TGSMUN has grown into Hyderabad's most spirited stage for young diplomacy.

Our delegates don't just debate — they research, caucus, draft and build consensus under pressure, developing the confidence and empathy that real-world diplomacy demands.

Whether it's your first placard or your fifteenth gavel, there is a seat across our senior and junior committees with your name on it.

From the Secretary-General

Dear Delegates,

It is my privilege to welcome you to the fifth edition of the Gaudium School Model United Nations. For five years this conference has been built, argued over and gavelled into being entirely by students — and every August it hands the floor back to you.

Over two days you will represent people whose convictions may be nothing like your own. That is the whole exercise. The best delegates in this room will not be the loudest; they will be the ones who research relentlessly, listen carefully, and find the sentence a divided committee can finally agree to sign.

This year our theme — Dialogue over Division — asks each of us to listen before we speak, and to seek common ground before claiming victory. I look forward to watching you rise to that challenge.

SG
Sneha Kolluri Secretary-General · TGSMUN 2026
