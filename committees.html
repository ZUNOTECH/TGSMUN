TGSMUN 2026 Committees & Agendas

Seven rooms,
seven world stages.

Classic UN debate, Indian political theatre, continuous crisis and a free press. Background guides reach registered delegates two weeks before the first gavel.

C·01

UNGA — DISEC

United Nations General Assembly · First Committee

Agenda

Regulation of autonomous weapons systems and AI in modern warfare.

As lethal autonomous weapons move from science fiction to the battlefield, can the international community agree on meaningful human control? Delegates weigh military necessity against humanitarian law — and draft a framework the great powers might actually sign.

Beginner Friendly
Double delegation~50 delegates
Guide — Soon
C·02

UNSC

United Nations Security Council

Agenda

The situation in the Arctic: sovereignty, security and shipping lanes.

Polar ice retreats; trade routes and untapped resources redraw the map of great-power competition. In a fifteen-member chamber where every veto matters, delegates navigate territorial claims, militarisation of the High North and the rights of indigenous communities.

Advanced
Single delegation15 delegates
Guide — Soon
C·03

UNHRC

United Nations Human Rights Council

Agenda

Protecting the rights of climate refugees and displaced populations.

No treaty yet recognises the person displaced by a drowning coastline. The Council examines the legal vacuum around climate-induced displacement, burden-sharing between North and South, and pathways to a binding protection framework.

Intermediate
Single delegation~40 delegates
Guide — Soon
C·04

WHO

World Health Organization

Agenda

Equitable access to healthcare technology in the Global South.

From mRNA platforms to AI diagnostics, medicine's newest tools remain concentrated in the wealthiest nations. Delegates debate patent flexibility, technology transfer and local manufacturing — what does "health for all" mean when innovation has a price tag?

Beginner Friendly
Single delegation~40 delegates
Guide — Soon
C·05

AIPPM

All India Political Parties Meet

Agenda

Deliberation on the One Nation One Election framework.

India's most spirited committee. Portraying leaders across party lines, delegates clash over simultaneous elections — federalism, cost, voter behaviour, constitutional amendment — in a format that rewards rhetoric, wit and razor-sharp political knowledge.

Intermediate
Portfolio-based~35 delegates
Guide — Soon
C·06

Crisis

Continuous Crisis Committee

Agenda

Classified — briefings released 48 hours before the conference.

A fast-moving simulation where the world changes every hour. Directives, covert operations and midnight crises test your ability to think on your feet. For experienced delegates who thrive under pressure. Expect the unexpected.

Advanced
Portfolio-based~25 delegates
Guide — Soon
C·07

Int'l Press

International Press Corps

Mandate

Journalism, photography and caricature across all committees.

The eyes and ears of the conference. Reporters file dispatches from every room, photographers catch the moments that matter, caricaturists skewer the day's diplomacy — and the Press publishes a newsletter at each day's close.

Beginner Friendly
Journalist / Photographer~20 members
