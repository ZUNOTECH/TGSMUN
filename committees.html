TGSMUN 2026

Committees & Agendas

Seven committees spanning classic UN debate, Indian political simulation, crisis and press. Background guides are released to registered delegates two weeks before the conference.

UNGA
General Assembly — DISEC

Regulation of autonomous weapons systems and AI in modern warfare

The First Committee confronts one of the defining security questions of the decade: as lethal autonomous weapons move from science fiction to the battlefield, can the international community agree on meaningful human control? Delegates will weigh military necessity against humanitarian law, and draft a framework the great powers might actually sign.

UNSC
United Nations Security Council

The situation in the Arctic: sovereignty, security and shipping lanes

As polar ice retreats, new trade routes and untapped resources are redrawing the map of great-power competition. In a fifteen-member council where every veto matters, delegates must navigate overlapping territorial claims, militarisation of the High North and the rights of indigenous communities.

UNHRC
United Nations Human Rights Council

Protecting the rights of climate refugees and displaced populations

No international treaty yet recognises the person displaced by a drowning coastline. The Council will examine the legal vacuum around climate-induced displacement, burden-sharing between the Global North and South, and pathways to a binding protection framework.

WHO
World Health Organization

Equitable access to healthcare technology in the Global South

From mRNA platforms to AI diagnostics, the newest tools of medicine remain concentrated in the wealthiest nations. Delegates will debate patent flexibility, technology transfer and local manufacturing — asking what "health for all" means when innovation has a price tag.

AIPPM
All India Political Parties Meet

Deliberation on the One Nation One Election framework

India's most spirited committee. Portraying political leaders across party lines, delegates will clash over simultaneous elections — federalism, cost, voter behaviour and constitutional amendment — in a format that rewards rhetoric, wit and razor-sharp knowledge of Indian politics.

CCC
Continuous Crisis Committee

Agenda classified — briefings released 48 hours before conference

A fast-moving simulation where the world changes every hour. Directives, covert operations and midnight crises will test delegates' ability to think on their feet. Recommended for experienced delegates who thrive under pressure. Expect the unexpected.

IP
International Press

Journalism, photography and caricature across all committees

The eyes and ears of the conference. Reporters file dispatches from every committee, photographers capture the moments that matter, and caricaturists skewer the day's diplomacy. The IP publishes a conference newsletter at the close of each day.
